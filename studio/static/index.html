CONTROLLED EVALUATION · V1

A score is not a discovery.

Compare fixed MOVE, random actions, a directional foraging heuristic and the saved champion, with no additional training. Five prespecified held-out world seeds. One founder per trial, shared environment configuration and a maximum 300-tick budget.

Read the numbers responsibly

Identical initial seeds do not imply identical later food layouts: policies affect occupancy and reproduction. Results are descriptive per-seed outcomes, not proof of learning, cooperation or superiority. Training fitness is world-relative. A champion is available only after a generation has been evaluated.
